Terms of service and policies

Please read before ordering

PayPal and Cash App are the only accepted payment methods. Never pay anyone who messages you from a different account claiming to be me.

Payments and deposits
  • I accept PayPal and Cash App only. No checks, gift cards, crypto or trades.
  • All prices are in US dollars. I send a quote first.
  • Work starts after I receive a 50% deposit. The other 50% is due before I send or ship the final piece.
  • You cover any payment fees or currency conversion charges.
  • Payments must come from an account in your own name or one you are allowed to use.
Shipping, tracking and returns
  • Physical items (keychains, ita bags and prints) ship after the final payment clears. Shipping costs are added to your quote.
  • Every shipped order gets a tracking number. It shows on the Track page as soon as I add it.
  • You are responsible for giving me a correct address. I cannot refund a package that was delivered to the address you gave me.
  • If a package is lost or damaged in transit, tell me within 14 days of the delivery date and I will work with you and the carrier to fix it.
  • Handmade items vary slightly from photos. Wood grain and leather tone are always a little different.
  • To return something, message me first. After you verify your order on the Track page, you will see my return address there. Items sent back without contacting me first may not be accepted.
Station meetups
  • Meetups are only available within the Houston, Pearland and Galveston area. I cannot travel farther than that, so please choose mail if you are outside it.
  • I confirm the station, day and time with you by email before we meet.
  • We meet in a busy public place. Please do not share a private address for meetups.
  • If we miss each other, I can ship the item instead. Shipping costs are added.
Refunds and cancellations
  • Before I start the sketch or materials: full refund of your deposit.
  • After the sketch is approved or materials are cut: the deposit is non-refundable.
  • After line art, coloring or crafting is underway: no refunds, though I may offer a partial one.
  • If I have to cancel for any reason, you get a full refund of everything you paid.
  • Finished and delivered work is not refundable.
Chargebacks and disputes

Please message me first if something is wrong, and I will work with you to fix it. Opening a chargeback or payment dispute after receiving finished work is a breach of these terms. I may share proof of the order, progress shots, tracking and delivered files with the payment provider, and I will not offer further commissions to you.

Revisions and changes
  • Drawn art: unlimited changes at the sketch stage and 2 small changes at the color stage.
  • Handmade items: changes are only possible before I cut or stamp the material.
  • Big changes after approval, like a new pose, outfit or design, are charged as a new commission or an extra fee.
  • I fix my own mistakes for free at any stage.
Turnaround and communication
  • The times on the menu are estimates and start after your deposit clears.
  • Rush orders cost 25% extra and depend on my schedule.
  • I reply within 48 hours. If you go quiet for 14 days, I may pause or close your order and the deposit stays with me.
  • I will tell you if life gets in the way and your piece will be late.
Usage rights and credit
  • You may use your commission for personal use, like profile pictures, wallpapers and posting online with credit.
  • Commercial use, such as merch, ads, books, games or streams that earn money, needs a commercial license. Ask me for a quote.
  • I keep the copyright to every piece. You own your characters and designs.
  • Do not resell, trace, edit, redistribute or claim my art as your own. Please credit me when you post it.
  • Do not use my art for NFTs, crypto, or to train or prompt any AI model.
Portfolio and sharing

I may post your finished piece and work-in-progress images on my portfolio, social media and in promo. Tell me before I start if you want it kept private. Private orders may cost a little extra. I never share your personal details.

What I make and what I do not
  • I make original characters, OCs and fursonas, cute and spooky designs, couples, friends and pets, and fan art of characters that are free to use.
  • I may turn down any request without giving a reason.
  • I do not make hateful, discriminatory, graphic, explicit or illegal content, or anything involving real people in harmful ways.
  • You must have permission to use any reference images you send.
  • Please be kind. Harassment, pressure or abusive messages will end the order with no refund.
Privacy

I only collect the name, email, address or station and payment details needed to complete your order. I do not sell or share them, except with the payment provider and the shipping carrier. Addresses are only visible to me. I keep order records for tax purposes and delete personal details when they are no longer needed.

Last updated November 2026. I may change these terms. The version posted when you pay your deposit is the one that applies to your order.
